Shared Wax Suite Agreement

The Collaborative Vision

We are building the waxing department of Cio Bela together. This agreement is a living document intended to ensure that our shared suite remains professional, hygienic, and inspiring for every artist who works within it. As we grow, we will refine these standards as a team.

1. Professionalism & Aesthetic

  • Suite Presentation: Each wax artist is responsible for keeping their designated area organized, clean, and visually aligned with the salon's brand between and after all appointments.
  • Atmosphere: We agree to maintain a professional tone and a welcoming, private environment for all clients.
  • Personal Branding: You are encouraged to market your unique style and approach; however, any permanent changes to the physical suite must be discussed with the collective.
  • Client Privacy: The wax suite is an intimate service environment. We commit to ensuring client privacy and dignity at all times — including proper draping, controlled suite access during services, and discretion in all client communications.

2. Shared Suite Stewardship

  • Common Areas: The restroom and waiting area are shared. We agree to "leave it better than we found it" to ensure a high-end experience for every guest.
  • Wax Residue & Cleanliness: Wax residue on surfaces, warming units, or floors must be addressed immediately after each client. It is each artist's responsibility to remove all wax drips, strips, and debris from shared equipment before the next artist uses the suite.
  • Turnover: Every wax artist is responsible for fully sanitizing the wax table, warming unit, and surrounding surfaces between every appointment. The suite must be left in ready-to-use condition for any artist who follows.
  • Warmer Etiquette: If you are the last artist using a shared wax warmer for the day, it is your responsibility to turn it off, cover it, and leave it clean for the next session.

3. Logistics & Access

  • Entry: Each artist will have keyed access. We agree to ensure the building is fully secured and all non-essential lights and equipment are turned off when the last person leaves.
  • Client Flow: We will respect the privacy of the floor. If a client arrives early, we will guide them to the designated waiting area — not the wax suite — until the artist is ready to receive them.
  • Guest Policy: Wax artists must communicate their individual policies regarding children or extra guests to their clients in advance. Due to the intimate nature of waxing services, additional guests are generally discouraged in the suite during treatments.
  • Friends & Family: To maintain a focused, professional environment, friends and family members of wax artists are not permitted to occupy the salon unless they are present to receive a service.

4. Supplies & Inventory

  • Storage: Each artist is allocated their own designated space for personal supply storage. Items left on shared surfaces or in common areas may be moved to ensure suite safety and cleanliness.
  • Shared Equipment: Any shared warming units, trolleys, or suite furniture must be treated with care and returned to their designated positions after each use.
  • Linen & Disposables: Each artist is responsible for managing their own wax strips, disposable liners, and table paper. Shared laundry facilities are available for linens; please move loads promptly.

5. Conflict & Resolution

Because we are building this together, we agree to open and honest communication. Any friction regarding suite scheduling, supplies, noise, or shared space will be handled privately and professionally between the parties involved, or brought to management for a group discussion.

6. Cancellations & Calling Out

As an independent business owner, you are fully responsible for managing your own schedule and client communications. If you are unable to come in, it is your sole responsibility to contact your clients directly and in a timely manner to inform them and arrange rescheduling. The salon is not responsible for notifying your clients on your behalf. Consistent failure to handle cancellations and rescheduling in a professional and timely manner reflects on the salon as a whole. The salon reserves the right to terminate your rental agreement if this standard is not upheld on a consistent basis.

7. Texas Compliance (TDLR)

We collectively agree to keep the suite "Inspection Ready" at all times. Please ensure a copy of your current license and a government-issued photo ID are available for inspection at all times. Proper labeling of "Clean" versus "Dirty" implements and strict adherence to single-use policies are non-negotiable standards in our shared environment.

Daily Stewardship Checklist

Opening Procedures — "The Set Up for Success"

  • Lights & Atmosphere: Turn on all suite lighting. Ensure the music or ambient playlist is at an appropriate, relaxing volume.
  • Wax Warmers: Turn on warmers at least 20–30 minutes before your first appointment to ensure wax reaches the correct working temperature.
  • Table Prep: Verify the wax table is fully sanitized, fitted with a clean liner or paper, and all supplies are stocked and within reach.
  • Waiting Area Check: Straighten any shared seating or décor. Ensure the check-in area is tidy and welcoming.
  • Restroom Check: Verify it is clean, stocked with toilet paper and hand towels, and the trash is empty.

Between Every Client — "The Turnover"

  • Table Reset: Remove and dispose of all used table paper and disposable liners immediately. Replace with fresh coverings before the next client.
  • Surface Sanitation: Wipe down the wax table, trolley, and any shared surfaces with an EPA-registered, hospital-grade disinfectant after every single appointment.
  • Wax Residue Removal: Clear all wax residue from the warmer rim, applicator holder, and immediate work area. Do not leave residue for the next artist.
  • Implements: Remove all used or contaminated tools from the work surface immediately. Single-use items go directly in the lined waste bin — never set aside for reuse.
  • Hand Hygiene: Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water or use 70% alcohol-based sanitizer before receiving the next client.

Throughout the Day — "The Flow"

  • Waste Management: Empty your personal suite waste bin before it becomes full. Double-bag any wax-saturated or bodily-fluid-contaminated waste.
  • Warmer Monitoring: Check wax temperature periodically throughout the day to maintain a safe, consistent working temperature and prevent overheating.
  • Linen Rotation: If a load of towels or linens is dry, fold them and put them away; move the next load to the dryer.

Closing Procedures — "The Clean Slate"

  • Warmer Shutdown: Turn off all wax warmers. Wipe the exterior of each unit clean. Cover warmers if applicable.
  • Full Surface Wipe-Down: Perform a final sanitation of the wax table, trolley, and all hard surfaces touched during the day.
  • Waste Disposal: Empty the suite waste bin into the main dumpster. Replace with a fresh liner.
  • Linen Check: Confirm all soiled linens are in the laundry. No used linens should remain in the suite overnight.
  • Supplies Stocked: Restock any low supplies (wax strips, disposable liners, table paper) so the suite is ready for the next artist who opens.
  • Final Walkthrough: Lights off, suite is clean, warmer is off, and the front door is locked.
Document 03

Texas Safety & Compliance Protocols

1. Licensing & ID (Texas Law)

  • Visibility: Your current Texas Esthetician or Cosmetology license must be displayed at your workstation in public view at all times.
  • Identification: You must have a government-issued photo ID available at the salon for inspection by TDLR at any time.
  • Updates: You are responsible for notifying the Owner of any license renewals to keep our master Salon Renter List current.

2. Client Intake & Contraindications

  • Pre-Service Consultation: Every new client must complete a client intake form prior to their first service. This form must document current medications, skin conditions, and any known sensitivities or contraindications.
  • Contraindication Screening: You are responsible for refusing or modifying service for any client presenting with contraindications including, but not limited to: use of retinoids or Accutane, sunburned or broken skin, active rashes or skin infections, or recent cosmetic procedures in the area to be waxed.
  • Patch Tests: A patch test is strongly recommended for all new clients and any client who has changed their skincare routine or medications since their last visit.

3. Infection Control & Sanitation

  • Hand Hygiene: Wash hands with soap and water or use a 70% alcohol-based sanitizer before and after every client, without exception.
  • Surface Disinfection: All non-porous surfaces — including the wax table, trolley, and warming unit exterior — must be cleaned and disinfected with an EPA-registered, hospital-grade disinfectant between every client.
  • Single-Use Strict Policy: All wax applicators (sticks, spatulas) are strictly single-use and must be discarded immediately after one application. Double-dipping applicators into the wax pot is a TDLR violation and a contamination risk.
  • Table Coverings: Fresh disposable table paper or a clean linen must be used for every single client. Used coverings must be removed and disposed of immediately after each appointment.
  • Wax Pot Integrity: If a used applicator has come into contact with a wax pot, the entire pot must be discarded. Contaminated wax may not be used on any other client under any circumstances.

4. Wax Temperature Safety

  • Temperature Testing: Always test wax temperature on your own inner wrist before applying to a client. Hard wax should feel comfortably warm, never hot or painful.
  • Burn Prevention: Never apply wax that is too hot. Client burns are a serious liability. When in doubt, allow the wax to cool and test again before proceeding.
  • Warmer Maintenance: Wax warmers must be cleaned regularly to prevent old wax buildup, which can affect temperature consistency and contaminate fresh wax.

5. Chemical Safety

  • SDS Binder: All artists must provide a Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for any professional wax products or chemical preparations they bring into the salon.
  • Pre- and Post-Care Products: Any pre-wax cleansers, oils, or post-wax soothing treatments used on clients must be professional-grade and applied in accordance with manufacturer guidelines.
  • Storage: All products must be stored in their original containers, properly labeled, and kept away from direct heat or open flames.

6. Blood or Skin Break Exposure Procedure

If a client's skin is broken or bleeding during a service, the following protocol must be followed immediately:

  1. Stop the service in the affected area immediately.
  2. Apply gentle pressure with a clean, sterile pad.
  3. Apply antiseptic to the affected area and cover if needed.
  4. Double-bag all contaminated single-use items before disposal.
  5. Discard the wax pot if any blood-to-wax contact occurred.
  6. Clean and disinfect the work surface and all tools before resuming service in an unaffected area or before the next client.
